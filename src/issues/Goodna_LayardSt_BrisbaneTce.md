## The problem

A commuter cyclist attempting to travel from Ipswich to Brisbane will likely find themselves on Brisbane Terrace in Goodna. If they are riding in the seperated bike lane they will encounter a sign directing them onto the road at the intersection of Layard Street and Brisbane Terrace. The danger arises from motorists turning out of Layard Street. They are likely only looking to their right. They are unlikely to see a cyclist in the on-road bike lane approaching contra to the flow of normal traffic.

## What we're asking for

- A quick fix is to remove that sign and install a zebra crossing so that pedestrians and cyclists cross Layard Street in way that does not put them in unexpected locations.
- A proper fix is to use the wide shoulders available and install a seperated active transport path from Wacol station through Goodna and connecting to the existing path on Woogaroo Street.

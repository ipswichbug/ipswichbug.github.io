## The problem

A person hit by a car at 50 km/h has about a 90% chance of dying. At 30 km/h, about a 90% chance of surviving. Yet the streets where Ipswich kids ride and scoot after school are posted at 50 or 60 km/h, and intersections are built with wide sweeping corners so cars don't have to slow down.

## What we're asking for

- Lower default speed limits on local residential streets and in town centres, in line with what many other Australian councils are now doing.
- Tighter corners at intersections so turning vehicles slow down and the crossing distance is shorter.
- Protected medians and raised crossings at busy pedestrian and bike crossing points.

## Why it matters

Street design is the single biggest lever council has over how people drive. Lectures don't change behaviour. Geometry does.

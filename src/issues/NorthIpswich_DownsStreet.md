## The problem

Bicycle lanes in North Ipswich are sporadic and disconnected. Where they disappear, narrow lanes force cyclists and motor vehicles into the same space with little warning. In one location approaching Delacy Street a traffic island even prevents overtaking and causes motorists to rapidly brake and change course. See the youtube video linked on this post.

These are residential streets where adults and children need to go about their lives getting to and from work and school, yet the speed limit is set to 60 km/h. At this speed there is little chance of avoiding a collision. Any collision that does occur is certain to be fatal. On Friday 10th July 2026 this is what happened.

"A crash has claimed the life of a cyclist days after he was critically injured in a collision with a car in North Ipswich.

The 50-year-old man was riding along Lennon Lane when he collided with a silver Hyundai i30 at the Pine Street intersections at around 5:30 on Friday afternoon.

He was rushed to Princess Alexandra Hospital in a critical condition but died from his injuries yesterday."

Ipswich residents are contantly calling for traffic congestion to be addressed yet the streets make it impossible to leave the car at home. Public transport doesn't always go where and when we need it. The best way to reduce traffic on the roads is to have less traffic on the roads yet active transport is difficult and appallingly unsafe. It's up to Ipswich City Council to show some initiative and address this. No other level of government is going to ride in like a knight in shining armour and save us from our own lack of town planning. 

## What we're asking for

- Ensure bike paths are continuous and connected.
- Reduce speed limits to 50 km/h or less when there is no service lane or shoulder on the road.
- Modify pedestrian refuges and traffic island so that they don't block cyclist.

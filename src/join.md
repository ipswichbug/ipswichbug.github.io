## Ways to help

You don't need to come to meetings to make a difference.

- **Report issues.** Send us the problem spots on your rides. See [report an issue](/issues/report/).
- **Respond to consultations.** When council or TMR ask for feedback, a dozen separate submissions count for a lot more than one. We'll tell you when and give you the key points.
- **Come on a ride.** Numbers on the road are the best advocacy there is.
- **Lend a skill.** Mapping, photography, writing, design, engineering, or just knowing how council works. All useful.
